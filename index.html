01 / OUR MISSION

Brave Frontier.
Recreated 1-for-1.

Our goal is a faithful, 1-for-1 recreation of the Brave Frontier server.

Decomp Frontier brings together fans and contributors working toward that goal. Our installer, tools, and database support the project, while our Discord gives everyone a place to connect, share findings, and help move the work forward.
